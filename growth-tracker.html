How to read this

Methodology and limitations

  • GDP measures domestic output; GVA measures value added before product taxes less subsidies. They are related but not interchangeable.
  • Real GDP uses constant 2022-23 prices; nominal GDP uses current prices. Their numerical difference is not presented as inflation.
  • Quarterly YoY growth compares a quarter with the same quarter one year earlier. Annual growth compares complete fiscal-year levels.
  • Growth rates are derived from positive official MoSPI levels and retain the raw crore values, formula and unrounded result.
  • Estimate and revision labels are those supplied by the official API; “Not stated by API” is used only when absent.
  • RBI projections are forward-looking and remain separate from MoSPI actuals. A matching verified actual supersedes a completed-quarter projection in the primary outlook.
  • Publication dates come from official releases. Operational verification time is stored and shown separately.
  • Sector growth rates do not simply add to headline GDP growth. Sectoral and expenditure views are omitted until compatible official identifiers and provenance can be validated.
  • One GDP release alone does not establish a market outlook or constitute investment advice.

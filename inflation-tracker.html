How to read this

Actual

A directly published monthly MoSPI observation.

Derived average

An arithmetic mean using all three official monthly YoY observations in a fiscal quarter.

Partial derived average

A quarter-to-date mean with fewer than three published months; it remains alongside any RBI full-quarter projection.

RBI projection

A full-quarter forward estimate from an RBI MPC resolution, not an observed inflation reading.

Methodology and limitations

  • CPI uses the official 2024=100 base and All-India Combined CPI (General) series.
  • YoY inflation compares the index with the same month one year earlier; displayed YoY rates are those reported by MoSPI.
  • Quarterly averages are arithmetic means of monthly YoY observations and show their observation count.
  • Rural and urban differences describe separate official series; they do not establish why inflation differs.
  • Only a complete three-month derived average may supersede a matching projection in the displayed outlook; a partial quarter-to-date average does not.
  • Publication dates come from official releases. Operational verification time is shown separately and never used as a publication date.
  • CPI alone does not predict markets, interest rates or an individual household’s experience.

The current 2024=100 CPI series begins in January 2026. Earlier-base observations are not mixed into this trend.

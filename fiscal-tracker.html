How to read this

Unaudited provisional actualOfficial cumulative account recorded before final audit.
Budget EstimateThe full-year amount proposed in the Union Budget.
Revised EstimateAn official in-year revision, shown only when validated.
Percentage of BEYTD provisional actual divided by the compatible full-year Budget Estimate.
Cumulative YTDTotal from 1 April through the reported month.

Methodology and limitations

Union Government only. Monthly figures are cumulative and unaudited provisional. Receipts and expenditure are seasonal, so percentage-of-BE is context rather than a linear target. No monthly deficit-to-GDP ratio is calculated. Borrowing and debt are omitted because they require separate definitions and sources. This is not investment advice.
